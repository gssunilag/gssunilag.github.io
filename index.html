Our new home

Geosciences Centre of Excellence

Commissioned on April 17, 2026 by Lagos State Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu, the Centre is a five-level, solar-powered learning and research hub delivered by SNEPCo, NNPC Ltd and NCDMB in partnership with UNILAG. It supports B.Sc., M.Sc. and Ph.D. training.
